61 — The Road to Government

Privacy Policy

Information about how the game uses and protects data.

Operator: Fisnik Krasniqi  ·  Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. About the game

61 — The Road to Government is an independent multiplayer political strategy game. Players can use the game without providing an email address or password. The app creates an anonymous guest identity to support gameplay and associate game activity with a participant.

2. Information we process

  • Guest identity and session: A generated account identifier, session tokens, and authentication information required to use the game.
  • Gameplay: Match participation, chosen characters, round actions, investments, campaign activity, territory results, scores, delegates, and match outcomes.
  • Technical information: Our infrastructure provider may process network data, IP addresses, timestamps and technical logs to deliver, troubleshoot and protect the service.

Other participants in a match may see information made public by the game's rules, including results and player status. Private round actions are intended to remain hidden until the applicable reveal or resolution.

3. Why we use this information

We process information to operate multiplayer matches, maintain guest sessions, apply game rules, calculate results, protect accounts and game integrity, and diagnose technical problems.

4. Service provider

The game uses Supabase to provide authentication, database storage, and server-side functions. The provider may process technical information as needed to deliver these services.

5. Retention and guest deletion

Guest identity and game data may remain available while required for multiplayer functionality and game history. The app includes a Delete Guest Data option under Settings.

When requested and accepted by the server, this process is designed to delete the guest authentication account and remove associated private game records. Account deletion is implemented using a server-side process, including a soft-deletion mechanism for authentication records. Deletion may be blocked during an active match.

Completed shared match results and non-private game history may be retained so other participants can access their completed matches. Some information may also remain temporarily in security logs or backups according to service-provider retention practices.

Important: Deleting a guest account should not be understood as an immediate deletion of every historical match record or every backup copy.

6. How to delete your guest data

Open Settings → Delete Guest Data and follow the confirmation steps. If the guest is participating in an active match, complete or leave the match before trying again. The app may create a new guest identity if you continue playing after deletion.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request information about your data and request access, correction or deletion. You can use the in-app deletion feature or contact the operator using the address below.

8. Children's privacy

The game is not specifically directed at children. We do not ask users to provide their birth dates. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's use of the game can contact us.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date shown above indicates when this page was last updated.
